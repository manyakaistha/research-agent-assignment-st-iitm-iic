## Snapshot
- Wipro Limited is an AI-powered technology services and consulting company that provides innovative solutions for digital transformation. [S1]
- For the quarter ended September 30, 2026, Wipro reported gross revenue of ₹247.5 billion ($2,583.0 million), an increase of 1.1% quarter-over-quarter and 9.1% year-over-year. [S1]
- Net income for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, was ₹32.0 billion ($333.9 million), a decrease of 4.7% quarter-over-quarter and 1.9% year-over-year. [S1]
- The IT services operating margin for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, was 16.5%, which expanded by 0.5% quarter-over-quarter but decreased by 0.2% year-over-year. [S1]

## Bull case
- Wipro's CEO and Managing Director stated that strategic investments are enhancing the company's ability to win in new markets, add new clients, and grow existing relationships. [S1]
- Management expects IT Services business segment revenue for the quarter ending December 31, 2026, to be in the range of $2,571 million to $2,625 million, translating to a sequential guidance of (-)1.5% to (+)0.5% in constant currency. [S1]

## Bear case
- Total Bookings for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, were $2,595 million, a decrease of 22.9% quarter-over-quarter in constant currency, with large deal bookings decreasing by 58.6% quarter-over-quarter in constant currency. [S1]
- Net income for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, decreased by 4.7% quarter-over-quarter and 1.9% year-over-year. [S1]
- Voluntary attrition was 13.8% on a trailing 12-month basis for the period ended September 30, 2026. [S1]
