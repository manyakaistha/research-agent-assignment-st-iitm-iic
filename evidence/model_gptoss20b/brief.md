## Snapshot
- Sarvottam Cables Ltd manufactures power cables, wires and conductors. [S3]
- Revenue from operations in Q1 FY27 was ₹1,248 crore, up 18.0% YoY. [S3]
- Profit after tax was ₹82 crore, up 5.9% YoY. [S3]
- EBITDA margin was 11.2% in Q1 FY27, down 170 bps from 12.9%. [S3]

## Bull case
- Management maintains FY27 revenue growth guidance of 15–17% and plans to double data‑centre cable sales in two years. [S2]
- The new Bharuch plant will add ~30% capacity and is expected to begin commercial production in Q3 FY27, with an order book of ₹3,900 crore. [S3]

## Bear case
- Operating margins contracted by 170 bps due to a ~14% rise in copper prices during the quarter. [S1]
- Accounts receivable days rose from 78 to 96, mainly because of slower payments from two large state utility customers. [S2]
- The company received a ₹46.3 crore GST demand order, which it plans to appeal and does not expect material impact. [S7]

## Open questions
- Revenue conflict: Official filing shows ₹1,248 crore vs news report of ₹1,428 crore; growth 18.0% vs 35%. [S3] [S1]
- Promoter pledge difference: 4.1% of promoter holding in Jun‑2026 vs 6.0% in Mar‑2026 and 11.5% in Jun‑2025. [S8]
- Accounts receivable days increase: From 78 to 96; how will this affect cash conversion? [S2]
