## Snapshot
- Sarvottam Cables Ltd manufactures power cables, wires and conductors. [S3]
- Revenue from operations ₹1,248 crore, up 18% year‑over‑year. [S3]
- Profit after tax ₹82 crore, rising 5.9% from the prior year. [S3]
- EBITDA margin 11.2%, a decline of 170 basis points from 12.9%. [S3]

## Bull case
- Management maintains FY27 revenue growth guidance of 15‑17% and expects Bharuch plant capacity to rise 30% from Q3 FY27. [S2] [S3]
- Data‑centre cables now account for about 9% of domestic revenue and may double in two years. [S2]

## Bear case
- Copper prices rose nearly 14%, causing operating margin to drop 170 basis points. [S1] [S3]
- Receivable days increased to 96 from 78, driven by slower payments from two large state utilities, with no defaults yet. [S2]
- The company received a ₹46.3 crore GST demand order and plans to file an appeal, though it expects no material impact. [S7]

## Open questions
- Revenue conflict: official filing reports ₹1,248 crore vs news report ₹1,428 crore. [S1] [S3]
- Promoter pledge: 4.1% as of June 2026 vs 35% reported in March 2024. [S8] [S4]
- Will the increase in receivable days translate into cash collection delays or defaults? [S2]
