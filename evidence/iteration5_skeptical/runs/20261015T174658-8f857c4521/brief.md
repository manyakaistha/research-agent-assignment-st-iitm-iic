## Snapshot
- Sarvottam Cables Ltd manufactures power cables, wires and conductors. [S3]
- Q1 FY27 revenue from operations was ₹1,248 crore, up 18.0% YoY from ₹1,058 crore. [S3]
- Profit after tax for Q1 FY27 was ₹82 crore. [S3]
- EBITDA margin fell to 11.2%, down 170 basis points from 12.9% in Q1 FY26. [S3]

## Bull case
- The order book stood at ₹3,900 crore as of 30 June 2026, up from ₹3,150 crore a year earlier. [S3]
- Management maintains FY27 revenue growth guidance of 15‑17% and expects to pass on higher copper costs over the next two quarters. [S2] [S3]

## Bear case
- Operating margins contracted by 170 basis points to 11.2% as copper prices rose about 14% in the quarter. [S1] [S2]
- Receivable days increased from 78 to 96 days, driven by slower payments from two large state utilities. [S2]
- The company received a GST demand of ₹46.3 crore for FY2020‑21 and FY2021‑22; it plans to appeal and does not expect material impact. [S7]

## Open questions
- Revenue conflict: the company reported ₹1,428 crore (35% growth) in a news article (S1) versus the official filing figure of ₹1,248 crore (18% growth) (S3). [S1] [S3]
- Promoter pledge discrepancy: a 2024 news report said 35% of promoter holdings were pledged (S4), while the latest shareholding filing shows only 4.1% pledged as of June 2026 (S8). [S4] [S8]
- Can the company successfully pass on higher copper costs as anticipated, given margin pressure observed in the quarter? [S2] [S3]
