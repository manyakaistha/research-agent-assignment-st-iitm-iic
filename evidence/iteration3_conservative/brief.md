## Snapshot
- Sarvottam Cables Ltd is a manufacturer of power cables, wires and conductors. [S3]
- Q1 FY27 revenue was ₹1,248 crore, up 18.0% YoY. [S3]
- EBITDA margin fell to 11.2% (down 170 bps) and profit after tax was ₹82 crore. [S3]

## Bull case
- Order book stood at ₹3,900 crore as of 30 June 2026, up from ₹3,150 crore a year earlier. [S3]
- The Bharuch plant, adding ~30% capacity, is slated for commercial start in Q3 FY27. [S3]

## Bear case
- Operating margins contracted to 11.2% after a 170‑bp decline due to higher copper prices. [S1]
- Receivable days rose to 96 from 78, indicating slower cash collection. [S2]
- The company faces a GST demand of ₹46.3 crore and is appealing the order. [S7]

## Open questions
- Revenue conflict: S1 reports ₹1,428 crore (+35%) while the official filing (S3) shows ₹1,248 crore (+18.0%). [S1] [S3]
- Promoter pledge: a 2024 report said 35% of holdings were pledged, whereas the latest filing (Jun 2026) shows only 4.1% pledged. [S4] [S8]
- How will the rise in receivable days and the timing of the Bharuch plant affect cash flow and execution? [S2] [S3]
