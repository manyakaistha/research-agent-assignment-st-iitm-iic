## Snapshot
- Sarvottam Cables manufactures power cables, wires and conductors; results are for Q1 FY27 (quarter ended 30 June 2026). [S3]
- Official consolidated revenue was ₹1,248 crore, up 18.0% year-on-year. [S3]
- Profit after tax was ₹82 crore, up 5.9% year-on-year. [S3]
- EBITDA margin was 11.2%, down 170 bps from 12.9% a year earlier. [S3]

## Bull case
- Order book stood at ₹3,900 crore as of 30 June 2026, up from ₹3,150 crore a year ago. [S3]
- The company says the Bharuch plant, adding about 30% to cable capacity, is on track for Q3 FY27 commercial production; the MD maintains FY27 revenue growth guidance of 15–17%. [S3] [S2]

## Bear case
- EBITDA margin fell 170 bps to 11.2% on higher copper prices; the MD expects to pass costs on within two quarters. [S3]
- Receivable days rose from 78 to 96 over the past year, mainly due to two state utility customers. [S2]
- A CGST demand of ₹46.3 crore (FY20-21 and FY21-22 ITC) was received; the company intends to file an appeal. [S7]

## Open questions
- Revenue conflict: the news report (S1) states ₹1,428 crore (+35%); the official filing (S3) states ₹1,248 crore (+18.0%) for Q1 FY27; the official figure is preferred. [S1] [S3]
- A historical news report (March 2024, S4) stated 35% of promoter holding was pledged; the latest NSE filing (June 2026, S8) shows 4.1% of promoter holding pledged. [S4] [S8]
- Will the planned CGST appeal be filed and resolved, and will receivable days return toward the prior 78-day level? [S7] [S2]
